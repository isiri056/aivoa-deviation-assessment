Critical Process Parameter: Drying Chamber Product Temperature

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. INCIDENT DESCRIPTION & SEQUENCE OF EVENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During the routine fluidized bed drying cycle of Paracetamol API batch API-260924,
the approved validated recipe operating temperature range is 70°C to 75°C.

At approximately 14:22 hrs, drying temperature sensor TE-204 recorded an upward
temperature excursion reaching 82°C. The temperature remained out of validated
operating parameters (between 78°C and 82°C) for a duration of approximately 18 minutes.

The production operator immediately observed the audible high-temperature alarm on
the SCADA control console and initiated emergency cool air bypass flow to return the chamber
temperature back within the validated 70°C - 75°C range.

Preliminary root cause appears to be a transient proportional steam control valve
sticking issue on heat exchanger HX-102.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. IMMEDIATE CONTAINMENT ACTIONS TAKEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Heating cycle manually halted and diverted to ambient cooling purge.
2. Batch API-260924 (500 kg) immediately placed on PHYSICAL and SYSTEM QUARANTINE HOLD.
3. Yellow Quarantine tags affixed to FBD-02 product discharge IBCs.
4. QA On-call deviation notification issued within 1 hour per SOP-QMS-402.
5. In-process composite sampling scheduled for thermal degradation assay and chromatographic
   purity profiling (p-aminophenol and related substance 4-nitrophenol analysis).
